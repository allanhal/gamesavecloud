gamesavecloud ships portable only.

Settings, sync state, local backups and your own recipes are stored in the
"gamesavecloud-data" folder next to gamesavecloud.exe rather than in %APPDATA%.
Copy the whole folder to another PC (or a USB stick) to take your setup with you.

Add a game the app does not know: drop a recipe .json into
gamesavecloud-data/recipes (the app's "Save recipe" button writes one for you).

There is no auto-update. Download a newer zip from
https://gamesavecloud.vercel.app/download and replace the files,
keeping the gamesavecloud-data folder.
